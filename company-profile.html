Since 2013, Trailer Solution has been designing and fabricating heavy-duty trailers from our facility in Ras Al Khor Industrial Area 2, Dubai — built for logistics fleets, transport companies and contractors across the UAE.

Precision Engineering

From Spec Sheet to Road

Seven stages, all under one roof at our Dubai facility.

01

Specification & Design

We start with the job the trailer has to do — cargo type, payload, route and the tractor unit it will run behind. Axle count, chassis length, deck height and coupling geometry all follow from that, not from a template. You approve the spec before any steel is cut.

02

Structural Steel Cutting

Main beams, cross members and gussets are cut to the drawing. Getting the profile right at this stage is what keeps the chassis straight later — a few millimetres of error here compounds across the length of the frame.

03

Chassis Fabrication & Welding

The main frame is jigged, squared and welded by our own fabricators. Load paths run continuously through the chassis so stress transfers into the structure rather than concentrating at a joint. Welds along the primary beams are checked as the frame goes together, not only at the end.

04

Suspension, Axles & Braking

Suspension and axles are mounted to the approved configuration, and the braking and air systems are plumbed in. Axle alignment is set at this stage — it is the single biggest factor in how evenly the trailer wears its tyres over the years that follow.

05

Surface Preparation & Paint

The frame is cleaned and prepared before coating. In UAE conditions — heat, humidity, coastal salt and construction dust — surface prep is what decides whether the finish is still intact in year five. Colour and livery are done to your requirement.

06

Bodywork & Fit-Out

Decking, sides, stake pockets, curtains, box panels, ramps or reefer insulation are fitted according to the build. Lighting, wiring, mudguards, landing legs and safety markings go on at this stage.

07

Inspection & Handover

The completed unit is checked over before it leaves the yard — structure, brakes, lighting, coupling and finish. You or your agent are welcome at the facility to inspect the finished trailer before it goes.
