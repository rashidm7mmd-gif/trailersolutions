Flatbed & Stake Body Trailers

The most versatile trailer we build. An open deck takes anything a forklift can place on it, and removable stakes let you box the load in when you need containment or strip them out when you need to load from the side.

Specification

Deck
Open flatbed, length to order
Sides
Removable stakes with mesh infill
Loading
Side, rear or overhead by crane
Typical use
General freight, building materials, palletised cargo

Every figure above is a starting point, not a fixed catalogue spec — we build to your requirement.

What it carries

Palletised freight, cement and block, timber, scaffolding, plant and general site materials. The same trailer handles a full mixed load one day and a single oversized item the next.

How we build it

Cross members are spaced for the load you actually carry rather than a catalogue default — a trailer hauling dense palletised goods needs different support to one carrying long lightweight material. Stake pockets are fabricated into the deck edge so the stakes sit square and stay square.

Specifying yours

Deck length, stake height and spacing, axle configuration and whether you want mesh, solid or no infill. Drop sides can be fitted if you load from the side regularly.
